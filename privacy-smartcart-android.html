Privacy Policy — SmartCart for Android

Leer en español

1. Introduction

SmartCart ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("the App").

SmartCart is published by NULL CLUB, based in Georgia. NULL CLUB is the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy and can be contacted at nullclub365@gmail.com.

2. Data Storage – On Your Device

Content you enter into the App—including meal plans, grocery lists and pantry items, nutrition logs, recipes you write, preferences and settings, and cooking history—is stored on your device. We do not upload this content to our servers or to any cloud service of ours; your phone's own backup may include it (see Data Backup). Limited usage and device information is sent to the third-party services described below.

You can delete all of it at any time under Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the App. Erasing also resets the analytics identifier Firebase uses for this installation.

3. Firebase Analytics and Crash Reports

To understand how the App is used and improve it, we use Google Firebase Analytics. It collects app usage events (for example, screens viewed, features used, and recipes started or cooked), an app instance identifier, and general device information such as device model, operating system version, app version, and country. We do not send your name, email address, or phone number to Firebase. Firebase processes this data under Google's privacy policies and keeps it for 14 months after your last use of the App. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Firebase Analytics only collects data after you agree in the consent form described in section 4.

Firebase Analytics also stores a few of your settings with these events: the App's language, whether you have Premium, the diets you turn on (such as vegetarian or gluten-free), and how many people you cook for. We use them to see how the App works for different groups of people.

If the App crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. A report contains the technical state of the App at the time of the crash (such as the code location and stack trace), the device model, operating system version, app version, and an installation identifier. It does not include your meal plans, lists, or other content you enter. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

4. Advertising

SmartCart displays ads through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process information for advertising purposes in accordance with Google's privacy policies. This includes limited information about your app usage, general device information, and your advertising identifier (which you can reset in your device settings).

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are shown; Firebase Analytics follows the same choice. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices for ads.

Under some US state privacy laws (such as California's), showing personalized ads can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out at any time under Settings → Privacy choices for ads where it is shown, or by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, we may send you meal preparation reminders based on your meal planner, expiry alerts for pantry items, cooking timer alerts, and occasional tips. Notifications are scheduled on your device, and their content is not sent to our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings or in the App's settings.

6. Location Data

SmartCart does not request or use your device's location. The App has no access to your GPS or location services. Google AdMob may estimate your approximate region (such as your country or city) from your IP address to show and measure ads, as described in section 4.

7. In-App Purchases

SmartCart Premium is not on sale on Android yet. When it is, the following applies.

SmartCart offers a Premium subscription through Google Play. All payment processing is handled by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies. We do not collect, store, or process your payment card information. Purchase records and subscription status are managed by Google Play. You can manage your subscriptions and billing information in Google Play → Subscriptions.

8. Recipe Images

Recipe images are delivered by jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), a public content delivery network, from SmartCart's image repository on GitHub. When you view recipes, your device requests these images from jsDelivr, which receives standard request information such as your IP address, as with any website. No other personal information is shared, and jsDelivr's privacy policy applies to these requests.

9. Permissions

The App may ask for these optional permissions: Notifications, for reminders, timers, and expiry alerts; and Alarms & reminders, so cooking timers ring on time. You can change them at any time in your device settings.

10. Data Backup

If you use the backup and restore feature, your data export file is created and stored on your device or a location you choose. We do not have access to these backup files. You are responsible for securing and managing your backup files.

If your phone's own backup is on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), it includes SmartCart's data, as it does for other apps. That backup is kept by Apple or Google under your account and their terms; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

11. Data Security

Since your content data is stored locally on your device, data security depends on your device's security measures. We recommend using device lock screens, keeping your OS updated, and regularly backing up your data using the App's backup feature. Analytics and ad data sent to Google services are protected by Google's security measures.

12. Data Deletion

You can delete your content data at any time by uninstalling the App, which removes all data the App stores on your device. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered unless you have a backup. Analytics and advertising data are processed by Google under its privacy policies; for any request about this data, contact us at nullclub365@gmail.com.

13. Your Rights & Choices

You control your content data because it is stored on your device: you can view, change, export, or delete it at any time. For data processed by Google services, you can limit personalized ads and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases: your consent for analytics and ads, which you can withdraw at any time under Settings → Privacy choices for ads without affecting earlier processing; our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and secure, for crash reports and for loading recipe images; and the performance of our contract with you, for Premium purchases.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at nullclub365@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

14. Third-Party Services

SmartCart uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own privacy policies: Google Firebase Analytics (analytics), Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), Google AdMob (advertising), Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent), Google Play (in-app purchases), and jsDelivr (recipe images). Please review their privacy policies for details: Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and jsDelivr (https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

15. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the age at which you can agree to online services on your own can be higher (up to 16, depending on the country); if you are below that age, please ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to the consent form. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information should contact us immediately.

16. Contact & Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, concerns about your privacy, or requests to exercise your rights, please contact us at nullclub365@gmail.com or use Contact Support in the App's settings. We aim to respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.

17. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date below and, if required, by displaying a notice in the App. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

If this Privacy Policy is translated, the English version prevails in case of any inconsistency.
