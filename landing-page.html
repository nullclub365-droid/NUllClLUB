Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add recipes to my meal plan? ▼
Open the Recipes tab, browse or search for a recipe, and tap "Add to Meal Plan." Choose the day and meal slot (breakfast, lunch, dinner). Done! Your ingredients automatically populate your grocery list.
Where is my data stored? ▼
All your data is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers. We never see your meal plans, recipes, or personal information. Your privacy is yours.
Can I backup my data? ▼
Yes! Go to Settings → Backup & Restore to export your data as a JSON file or import a previous backup. You have complete control over your data.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
Nope! SmartCart works immediately after download. No account, no login, no tracking. Just open and start planning.
What's the difference between Free and Premium? ▼
Free includes all core features: meal planning, grocery list, recipes, and nutrition tracking. Premium removes ads and unlocks additional features. Both versions get updates and support.
Does it work offline? ▼
Yes! SmartCart works completely offline. All your data is stored locally, so you can plan meals and access recipes anywhere, anytime.
Do you track my data or show me ads? ▼
We don't track you. Free tier includes tasteful ads; Premium removes them. We never sell your data, never harvest your meal information, and never share your information with third parties.
