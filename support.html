Frequently asked questions

The app is not loading or crashes. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app, update it to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play, and restart your phone. If it still happens, email us with the app name, your phone model, and your iOS or Android version.

Where can I read your privacy policies?

Open the Privacy page and choose the app you’re using. Each app has its own policy.

How do refunds and purchases work?

We never see your payment details, and refunds go through the store you bought from. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, pick the purchase and choose Request a refund.

What happened to Saidu?

We’re replacing Saidu with Friendzy, which has the same kind of party games plus more. If you already have Saidu, it keeps working, and we still answer questions about it.
