Delete your SmartCart account

Leer en español

SmartCart by NULL CLUB (iPhone and Android) lets you create an optional account by signing in with Apple or Google. You can delete that account at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open SmartCart, go to Settings → Account, tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to confirm with Apple or Google first. The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email nullclub365@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my SmartCart account”. If you used Apple’s “Hide My Email”, write from any address and include the relay address shown in your Apple ID settings (Sign in with Apple → SmartCart). We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account ID, name, email address, sign-in method and sign-in dates, stored in Google Firebase Authentication. Nothing is kept after deletion.

What is not affected

Your meal plans, grocery lists, pantry and other content are stored on your phone, not in your account, so deleting the account doesn’t remove them. You can erase them in the app under Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the app. Analytics and advertising data handled by Google are described in the privacy policy (Android).
