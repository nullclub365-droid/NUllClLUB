Privacy Policy — Friendzy

Friendzy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a party game app for iOS. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

Information stored only on your device

Friendzy stores the following preferences locally on your device using your device's built-in storage (Apple's UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server:

  • Whether you have completed the first-launch walkthrough.
  • Your haptic feedback preference (on/off).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information. We do not require you to create an account to use Friendzy.

Game content

All game prompts (questions, dares, words, trivia) are bundled inside the app itself. No prompt data is sent to or fetched from a remote server during gameplay.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Friendzy is free and shows ads between game sessions (never during active gameplay), served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and limit the frequency of ads. On first launch, iOS asks whether Friendzy may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your device's advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalised ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which games are played and how the app is used (for example, screens viewed and games started), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. This data is associated with a random app-instance identifier, not with your name or contact details. See the Firebase privacy information.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with Google only as described above, to serve ads and provide analytics and crash reporting.

3. Children

Friendzy is a party game app intended for use by groups of players. It is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us via the Support page and we will delete it.

4. Future updates

If a future version of Friendzy changes what data is collected, this privacy policy will be updated before those changes are released. The current policy is always available at this page and linked from within the app.

5. Your choices

  • You can clear all on-device preferences by deleting and reinstalling the app.
  • You can allow or deny tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or Friendzy's practices, please contact us via the Support page.
