Everything in Friendzy

  • Truth or Dare — Turn-based pick your fate. 500+ truths and 500+ dares.
  • Mafia — Full social deduction with role assignment, night phase, and detective mode.
  • Alias — Team-based word describing with timer, scoring, and skip penalties.
  • Trivia — Multiple choice, 1,000+ questions across categories, per-player scoring.
  • Never Have I Ever — 1,000+ prompts, no scoring, just chaos.
  • Would You Rather — Tough choices for the whole group.
  • Most Likely To — Point fingers and name names.
  • Hot Takes — Share your real opinion. No hiding.
  • Challenges — Physical dares for the brave.
  • 44 rule-based games — Classic word, memory, acting, and social games you play without the phone. We just give you the rules.

Meet Riot

Riot is Friendzy's fox mascot — chaotic, lovable, always hyped. Riot hosts every game, reacts to every prompt, and keeps the energy up when the room gets quiet. Every game night has a hype man. Yours is an animated fox with a spray can.

Privacy

No accounts. Your app preferences stay on your phone. If you play in a multi-phone room, your nickname and answers are shown only to the players in that room and deleted when the game ends. Friendzy is free and shows ads between games (never during one), served by Google AdMob, and uses Firebase to count which games get played and to report crashes. Read the full privacy policy.
