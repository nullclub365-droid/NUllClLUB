Everything in Friendzy

Pass one phone

  • Truth or Dare — Pick your fate: 500 truths and 500 dares.
  • Never Have I Ever — About 1,000 prompts. Find out who's done what.
  • Would You Rather — About 1,000 impossible choices for the whole group.
  • Most Likely To — About 1,000 prompts. Everyone points, nobody escapes.
  • Hot Takes — Share your real opinion. No hiding.
  • Challenges — Physical dares for the brave.
  • Alias — Describe the word before the timer runs out, in teams or solo.
  • Trivia — 900+ multiple-choice questions, with categories, difficulty and scores.
  • Mafia — Secret roles, night and day phases, doctor and detective, narrated by Riot.
  • Spy — Everyone knows the location except the spy. Find them before they figure it out.

Everyone plays on their own phone

The host opens a room and shares a four-letter code; friends join from their own phones, iPhone or Android. Rooms need an internet connection.

  • Comeback — Everyone writes the funniest answer, then votes. No names until the end.
  • Close Call — Guess the number, then bet on whose guess is closest.
  • Who Said That? — Guess who wrote each answer… or fool the whole room.
  • Word Association — Think alike to score points.
  • Mafia, Spy, trivia and Most Likely To can be played in a room too.

Made for game night

  • Game night recap — Share the winners and the funniest moments with your group chat.
  • Your crew — Host the same group again in one tap.
  • Tonight's Pack — Riot picks three games for your night, including road trip picks for the car.
  • 44 classic games — Word, memory, acting and social games you play without the phone. We just give you the rules.
  • Seasonal packs — New prompts for holidays through the year.
  • English and Spanish — The whole app, in both languages.

Meet Riot

Riot is Friendzy's fox mascot — chaotic, lovable, always hyped. Riot hosts every game, reacts to every prompt, and keeps the energy up when the room gets quiet. Every game night has a hype man. Yours is an animated fox with a spray can.

Privacy

No accounts. Your app preferences stay on your phone. If you play in a multi-phone room, your nickname and answers are shown only to the players in that room and deleted when the game ends. Friendzy is free and shows ads between games and between rounds of room games (never while anyone is answering), served by Google AdMob, and uses Firebase to count which games get played and to report crashes.
