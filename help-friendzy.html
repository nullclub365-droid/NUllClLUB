Friendzy Help & Safety

Friendzy is a party game for friends playing together in the same room. We want it to be fun for everyone, so here are straight answers to the questions and concerns people raise most often. If yours isn't here, email nullclub365@gmail.com. A real person reads every message.

Note: some answers below (accounts, friends, online status and Delete Account) describe features arriving in Friendzy's next update. Until then, there are no Friendzy accounts to delete or block from.

How fast do you reply?

Usually within a few days. Requests about your data (a copy, a correction or deletion) are answered within one month at the latest, and free of charge. Reports about safety or harmful content are looked at first.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Settings (⚙) → Delete Account. Your account, profile, friend code, friends list, requests and synced data are deleted straight away. If you can't get to the app (for example, you lost your phone), email us from the address you sign in with, or include your friend code, and we'll delete it for you. You never need an account to play: every game works without one. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.

What do you know about me?

Without an account, almost nothing that identifies you: your settings and game history stay on your phone. With an account, we keep your display name, the email Apple or Google shares with us (Apple lets you hide it), your friend code, friends and game counters. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or messages, there's no free-text chat, and we never sell your data. Ask for a copy at any time and we'll send it to you.

Is Friendzy safe for teens?

Friendzy is made for teens and adults playing together, and we've built it with teens in mind:

  • Accounts are 13+ only. Everyone is asked their birth month and year once, and we only keep the age group, on the phone.
  • No strangers. There's no public search and no free-text chat. Friends can only be added by code, QR or after playing together in a room, and names are filtered for offensive words.
  • Private by default. For players under 18, "Show when I'm online" starts switched off.
  • Teen-safe ads. Ads are limited to content rated for teens, are only shown between games, and are never personalised for players under 18.
  • Parental controls are respected. In US states that require it, we use Apple's and Google's age and parental-consent tools, and accounts close if a parent withdraws consent.

I'm a parent. My child is under 13 and made an account, or I have a question.

Email us with the subject "Friendzy child data" (and the friend code if you have it). We'll delete the account and its data within 30 days, usually much sooner, and we won't ask you to justify it. You're also welcome to ask what data we hold about your teen.

Someone is bothering me.

Tap their name in your Friends list and choose Block. Blocked players can't send you friend requests, room invites or shouts any more; our servers enforce this, not just the app. Use Report name for an offensive name, or Report a problem inside a room. If anything makes you feel unsafe, tell an adult you trust, and email us so we can act.

A dare or challenge felt unsafe.

Every prompt is optional. Anyone can skip with the → arrow, and nobody should feel pressured. We've reviewed our dares and removed ones with a risk of injury or choking (like cartwheels, forward rolls and arm wrestling), as well as dares that could expose other people's private messages. If you find one we missed, email us the exact wording: we'll remove or change it in the next update. Please only try what's safe for you and the space you're in.

A prompt was too adult, offensive or hurtful.

Friendzy's prompts are written for mixed groups of teens and adults: no sexual content, alcohol or drugs, and no diet or body-shaming talk. If something slipped through, email us the wording and we'll review it and change it in the next update.

Ads: too many, or one was inappropriate.

Ads only appear between games, never while you're answering, and are capped to teen-rated content. If you saw an ad that shouldn't be there, send us a screenshot and we'll block it in our ad settings. Watching a "rewarded" ad is always optional.

Do you track me or show personalised ads?

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, and for anyone under 18, Friendzy only shows non-personalised ads, even if you allow tracking. Players under 18 are never asked for tracking permission. Elsewhere, personalised ads are only used if you allow them: on iPhone through Apple's tracking prompt, and on Android through your phone's ad settings. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

I get too many notifications.

You can turn off room invites and "friends hosting" alerts separately in your Profile, or turn off all Friendzy notifications in your phone's settings. We don't send marketing emails.

It says accounts aren't available on my phone.

Accounts are for players aged 13 and over, based on the age entered when the app was first opened. If you're 13 or older and think something went wrong, email us and we'll help. All games can still be played without an account.

My bonus pack disappeared.

Unlocked packs are saved on the phone. If you sign in, they're kept on your account and come back on any phone, even after reinstalling. Without an account, reinstalling clears them; you can unlock them again in the app.

I'm still not happy.

Tell us. Reply to our email or write to nullclub365@gmail.com, and we'll do our best to put it right. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, or to Georgia's Personal Data Protection Service (personaldata.ge).
