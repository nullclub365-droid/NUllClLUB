Privacy Policy

NULL CLUB apps are designed with privacy in mind. We prefer keeping data on your device and avoid collecting more than we need. Each app has its own privacy policy so you can see exactly how your data is used (or not used) in that app.

Choose an app below to read the full policy.

  • SmartCart — Recipes, meal plans, grocery lists; your content stays on your device, with Firebase Analytics and AdMob ads (en español).
  • Saidu — Party games, being replaced by Friendzy; on-device preferences and AdMob (see policy for details).
  • GymFlow — Workouts and exercise library; local-first data, AdMob, UMP, and ATT. Terms of Use.
  • MemoroLearn — Study app; offline-first, local data only.
  • Friendzy — Party game app; preferences stay on your phone, room nicknames and answers are deleted when the game ends; AdMob ads between games, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Terms of Use.

For questions or data requests, see our Support page.
