# Reusable Prompt Template for Recipe Images

## Master Template

```
A high-end editorial food photograph of {DISH_NAME}, styled simply and beautifully. The dish is centered on a soft matte ceramic plate or bowl, sitting on a clean off-white surface (#F5F5F7) with subtle warm undertones. Soft, diffused natural daylight from the upper left, gentle shadows, no harsh highlights. Shot from a slight three-quarter overhead angle (about 45 degrees), shallow depth of field, the dish in crisp focus while the surface softly blurs at the edges. Minimal styling — only what naturally belongs with the dish. Warm and appetizing color grading, slightly desaturated, calm and premium mood, in the visual style of Apple's iOS marketing imagery, Bon Appétit, and Apple Fitness app meal cards. Plenty of negative space around the dish for cropping. Photorealistic, sharp, magazine quality. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand markings, no hands, no people, no utensils unless essential to the dish, no busy props.
```

## Usage

Replace `{DISH_NAME}` with the recipe name from the catalog.

## Key Characteristics

- **Style**: High-end editorial, Apple iOS aesthetic
- **Composition**: Three-quarter overhead angle, centered on ceramic plate
- **Background**: Clean off-white (#F5F5F7) with warm undertones
- **Lighting**: Soft, diffused natural light from upper left
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow (dish sharp, edges blurred)
- **Color Grading**: Warm, appetizing, slightly desaturated
- **Mood**: Premium, calm, magazine quality
- **Restrictions**: No text, logos, watermarks, hands, people, unnecessary utensils, busy props
- **Output**: Photorealistic, sharp, magazine quality

## Notes

This template ensures consistency across all generated recipe images while maintaining the premium, minimalist aesthetic aligned with Apple's design language.
