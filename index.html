House rules

  1. Open it and go.

    No account wall before you can try the thing. SmartCart and Friendzy work the moment they open.

  2. Your stuff stays on your phone.

    Recipes, meal plans, workouts and notes are saved on your device, not on our servers. Every app has its own privacy policy, written in plain language.

  3. One app, one job.

    We would rather make five small apps than one bloated one.

  4. A person reads the inbox.

    Write to nullclub365@gmail.com. A person on our team reads every message and replies, usually within a few days.
