## Executive Summary

SmartCart is a comprehensive meal planning and grocery management app with a strong technical foundation but **critical gaps in marketing positioning and user acquisition**. The app solves a real problem (meal planning friction) and has good competitive features (privacy-first, no account required, 415+ recipes), but lacks:

1. **Brand narrative** — Why choose SmartCart over competitors (Mealime, Eat This Much, etc.)?
2. **Landing page/web presence** — No discovery mechanism for potential users
3. **App Store Optimization** — Limited visibility strategy for iOS App Store
4. **Growth loops** — No referral, sharing, or viral mechanics
5. **Retention strategy** — No documented onboarding, email nurture, or engagement plan

**Estimated market opportunity:** 50M+ home cooks globally searching for meal planning solutions. Currently capturing: **0** (not discoverable).

**Recommended priority:** Fix positioning + web landing page + ASO before heavy growth marketing.
