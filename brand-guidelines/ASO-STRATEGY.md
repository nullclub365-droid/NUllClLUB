## 🎁 REVIEW STRATEGY (Critical for Launch)

**Problem:** "This app hasn't received enough ratings or reviews to display an overview"
**Impact:** New users can't see social proof → lower conversion

**Solution: Get 50 reviews in your first 2 weeks**

### Week 1: Beta Tester Reviews
- Ask your personal testers (friends, family, beta group)
- Email them: "SmartCart is live on the App Store! If you have a moment, please leave a review"
- Target: 15-20 reviews

### Week 2: Social Proof Campaign
- Post on Product Hunt (free, high-impact, 500+ installs possible)
- Post on Hacker News (if indie dev angle)
- Post on Indie Hackers (bootstrapped community)
- Tweet to your audience
- Target: 20-30 reviews from organic interest

### Week 3+: Ongoing Review Generation
- Implement in-app review prompt (iOS 14.5+)
  ```swift
  SKStoreReviewController.requestReview()
  ```
- Timing: After user completes first meal plan (natural moment)
- Expected conversion: 5-10% of active users → reviews

### Review Quality Management:
- Respond to every review (shows you care)
- Address negative reviews with fixes
- Thank 5-star reviewers
- Use feedback to improve app

**Target for Month 1:** 50+ reviews, 4.5+ rating
